Two adjustments learned from redrafting Google/Palladiio AI outreach on 2026-08-06:

**Re-engaging a thread Justin let go cold (he ghosted them, not the other way around):**
Own the silence plainly in one line rather than skipping straight back into pitch mode.
Drop any specific ask from the original message if it is now stale (e.g. a referral ask
tied to a job posting that may no longer be open) — reopen the relationship generally
instead of repeating the old ask verbatim. Keep the reconnect ask light (a short call),
not a renewed hard ask.

**CEO / founder / C-suite targets:**
Do not frame the DM as an informational interview request ("tell me your story", "hear
about your path", scheduling a call to learn about them personally) — this reads as
asking too much of a busy executive's time from a cold DM. Keep it short, state genuine
interest in the company's mission/product in one or two sentences, and make the ask the
lightest version available: stay connected / keep in mind for a future fit, rather than
booking their time for a conversation.

**Why:** Justin flagged both cases directly — Seth Teichman and Ria Jain threads went
cold after Justin stopped replying (screenshots showed his own unanswered follow ups),
and George Zhang at Palladiio AI is CEO, not CPO/Co-founder as originally listed, making
the standard "hear your story" ask framing wrong for that seniority level.

**How to apply:** Before drafting any outreach to a contact Justin has messaged before,
ask whether the thread went cold and who let it go quiet — a fresh pitch onto a stale
thread reads as if the earlier conversation never happened. Before drafting to a
C-suite/founder target, check seniority first and default to the lighter ask regardless
of what tier of contact this batch otherwise uses. See [[feedback_outreach_ask_type]] and
[[feedback_outreach_style_rules]] for the base DM rules these adjustments sit on top of.
