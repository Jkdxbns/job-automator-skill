Justin wants to deeply internalize every project — not just recall facts. Goal is to re-derive the same thought process used during development so he can explain projects to anyone with full depth and clarity, including "why this and not that" design decision questions.

**Why:** He used AI assistance during coding so needs to rebuild mental models from the code up.

**How to apply:**
- No summary/index files needed — he won't use them
- After analysis files are written, work through each project interactively: explain logic, ask him questions, go deeper on concepts
- Focus heavily on "why" reasoning: why this approach was chosen, what alternatives existed, why they were rejected
- Quiz format: ask him a question, let him answer, then correct/deepen
- One project at a time, thorough, not rushed
