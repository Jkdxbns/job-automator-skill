When giving Justin a drafted email or DM in chat, present it as plain paragraph text, never wrapped in a markdown blockquote (`>` prefix on each line).

**Why:** Justin flagged that the blockquote's rendered left bar (▎) gets copied along with the text when he selects and pastes the draft elsewhere, corrupting it. He showed a screenshot of the mangled paste as evidence.

**How to apply:** Any drafted outreach message, follow-up, or reply shown directly in chat — email or LinkedIn DM — should be plain text paragraphs with normal line breaks, no leading `>` on any line. This applies everywhere a draft is shown, not just outreach specifically.
